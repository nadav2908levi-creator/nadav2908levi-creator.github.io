Delete your Chartora account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chartora → Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

This deletes your progress, certificates and account from our cloud database and signs you out. Local data on the device is removed immediately.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Chartora account". We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted / kept

Deleted: name, email, account ID, learning progress, certificates. Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policy; cancel your subscription in Google Play separately.
