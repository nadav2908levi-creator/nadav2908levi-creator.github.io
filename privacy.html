Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the Chartora app ("the app") collects, why, and your choices. The app is operated by its developer, an Israeli sole proprietorship (licensed dealer) ("we").

What we collect

  • Learning progress (lessons completed, quiz and drill results, streak, settings) – stored on your device. If you sign in, it is also stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase) so it syncs across devices.
  • Account details – only if you create an account or sign in (optional): your name, email address and an account identifier. With "Continue with Google" these come from your Google account; with email sign-up you enter them yourself and your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication (stored only as a secure hash; we never see it). Your name is printed on certificates you earn.
  • Purchases – subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your payment card.
  • Advertising – free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data. In the EEA/UK we ask for consent first (Google's consent form), and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Premium users see no ads.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or financial account information. The app does not connect to any brokerage.

Why

To provide lessons and sync progress, to verify Premium, to show ads to free users, and to keep the app secure and working.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat), who process it on our behalf or under their own policies: Google, RevenueCat.

Retention and deletion

Progress on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Delete account and data. Cloud data is deleted when you delete your account (in the app or via this page), within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Changes
